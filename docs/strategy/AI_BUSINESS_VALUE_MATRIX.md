# Playmorrow AI — Business Value Matrix

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Status:** Active
**Purpose:** Scores every AI capability across 7 value dimensions to determine build priority.

---

## Dimensions

Each capability is scored 1 (minimal impact) to 10 (transformative impact) across 7 dimensions:

| Dimension | Definition | Example of 10 |
|---|---|---|
| **Save Time** | Reduces manual effort for players or studios | Semantic search replaces 8-click filtering — 30 seconds vs 3 minutes |
| **Improve Discovery** | Helps players find games they would never find otherwise | Cross-genre recommendations surface a hidden gem from a genre the player doesn't browse |
| **Increase Revenue** | Directly or indirectly increases platform GMV or studio earnings | Pricing intelligence leads to 15% higher conversion on optimized store pages |
| **Reduce Cost** | Reduces platform operational costs (moderation, support, infrastructure) | Spam auto-detection handles 80% of spam without human moderators |
| **Improve Trust** | Makes the platform more trustworthy for players, studios, or moderators | Explainable recommendations with "Why this?" build confidence that suggestions are genuine |
| **Improve Retention** | Makes users return to the platform more frequently | "What's New" digest pulls players back weekly to check followed game updates |
| **Creator Success** | Helps studios build sustainable businesses | Launch readiness scoring turns guesswork into data-informed go/no-go decisions |

## Key Insights

### 1. Discovery Capabilities Dominate

5 of the top 10 capabilities (and 4 of the top 5) are discovery-focused. This validates Playmorrow's core thesis: **AI exists primarily to solve the indie game discovery problem.** The platform's competitive advantage over Steam, itch.io, and Discord is AI-powered discovery. Every other AI capability — studio tools, moderation, marketplace intelligence — is secondary.

**Action:** Phase 6 must lead with discovery. M23 (Recommendation Engine, score 48) and M26 (Semantic Search, score 43) are not negotiable — they are the platform's future.

### 2. Studio Success = Platform Growth

Store Page Optimization (score 42) ranks just behind Recommendation Engine and Semantic Search. Studios that earn more stay on the platform, publish more content, and bring their communities. Studio AI tools are not "nice to have" — they are a growth flywheel.

**Action:** M25 (Studio Intelligence) must not be deprioritized. It is the platform's second strategic pillar after discovery.

### 3. Trust Features Have Broad But Diffuse Value

Content Moderation (37), Spam Detection (35), Fraud Detection (36) score solidly in Tier 2. They don't reach Tier 1 because their value is primarily **cost reduction + trust improvement** — critical for platform health but not directly revenue-generating or user-facing. At smaller scale (< 50K DAU), the cost savings are theoretical. At larger scale (> 100K DAU), they become essential.

**Action:** Build moderation/fraud capabilities in lockstep with platform growth. Don't over-invest before scale justifies it. M24 (AI Moderation) scored 6.7 on the Feature Evaluation Matrix — defer until DAU > 50K.

### 4. Low-Cost, High-Impact Quick Wins

These capabilities score Tier 2+ while being relatively simple to implement:

| Capability | Score | Complexity | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| "What's New" Digest | 33 | Low (LLM call + template) | High — directly drives retention |
| FAQ Generation | 35 | Low (semantic deduplication + summarization) | High — reduces support burden |
| Devlog Draft Text Generation | 35 | Low (prompt template + existing AIProvider) | High — immediate studio value |
| Review Quality Scoring | 32 | Low (classification + weighting) | Medium — improves trust gradually |

**Action:** Ship these as "bonus" items within existing milestones. They add value without adding weeks to the timeline.

### 5. Strategic Differentiators vs Table Stakes

**Strategic differentiators** (unique to Playmorrow, hard to replicate):
- Semantic Search (43) — no indie platform has this
- Recommendation Engine (48) — cross-genre, explainable
- Store Page Optimization (42) — no platform provides AI-powered store analysis
- Emotional Discovery (40) — mood-based browsing is unique

**Table stakes** (must have to compete, but not differentiating):
- Spam Detection (35) — every platform needs this
- Content Moderation (37) — required for community safety
- Text Generation (35) — increasingly common
- Fraud Detection (36) — mandatory for marketplace

**Action:** Allocate 70% of AI engineering resources to differentiators, 30% to table stakes. Differentiators win; table stakes prevent loss.

### 6. The Text Generation Paradox

Text Generation (devlog drafts) scores 35 — solid Tier 2. But its *strategic* value exceeds its numeric score. Why? Because it's the **first AI feature studios will use** — their entry point to trusting Playmorrow AI. If the first devlog draft is genuinely helpful, the studio explores Store Page Optimization, Wishlist Forecaster, and Sentiment Analysis. If the first draft is poor, they dismiss all AI features.

**Action:** Text Generation (M22a — Devlog Drafts, score 12.6 on Feature Evaluation Matrix) should ship early despite not being the highest-scoring capability. It's a trust-building gateway.

### 7. Capabilities That Should Be Free (Not Tier-Gated)

Some capabilities are so fundamental to specific features that they should be built as part of their parent capability, not as standalone items:

| Capability | Parent | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Query Intent Classification (22) | Semantic Search / NLU | Required for natural language search to function |
| Devlog Topic Classification (23) | Devlog Optimization | Feed ranking needs topic classification |
| Content Type Classification (25) | Content-Aware Feed Ranking | Feed ranking needs content types |
| Classification (general) (26) | Content Moderation | Moderation is classification |
| Inline Tooltip Definitions (25) | AI Chat Assistant | Chat provides definitions inline |
| "Because You Like" Explanations (27) | Recommendation Engine | Explanations are a sub-feature of recommendations |

**Action:** Don't schedule these as separate work items. Build them as sub-components of their parent capabilities.

### 8. Revenue Impact vs User Value Correlation

Capabilities that score high on both Revenue and Discovery:

| Capability | Discovery | Revenue | Both High? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recommendation Engine | 10 | 8 | Yes |
| Semantic Search | 9 | 5 | Partially — high discovery, moderate revenue |
| Store Page Optimization | 8 | 9 | Yes |
| Related Assets | 6 | 10 | Inverted — high revenue, moderate discovery |
| Bundle Suggestions | 6 | 9 | Inverted |
| Gaming Taste Profile | 9 | 7 | Yes |

**Insight:** The highest discovery-impact capabilities also drive the most revenue. This is not coincidental — better discovery → more games found → more wishlists → more purchases → higher GMV. The alignment between user value (discovery) and business value (revenue) means decisions rarely involve painful tradeoffs.

### 9. The Moderation Economic Threshold

Content Moderation and Spam Detection are the only capabilities where operational cost *reduction* is a primary value driver (scored 10 and 9 respectively on Reduce Cost). This creates an economic threshold:

- **At 10K DAU:** 1-2 human moderators handle all content. AI moderation saves ~$5K/month. Capability cost: ~$300/month. Net savings: $4.7K/month.
- **At 100K DAU:** 5-10 human moderators needed. AI moderation saves ~$25K/month. Capability cost: ~$800/month. Net savings: $24.2K/month.
- **Below 10K DAU:** AI moderation costs more than the human moderators it replaces.

**Action:** Build moderation capabilities when DAU crosses 10K. Before that, invest engineering time in discovery capabilities instead.

### 10. Capabilities Where "Improve Trust" Is the Primary Driver

Only a few capabilities have Trust as their highest-scoring dimension:

| Capability | Trust Score | Other High Scores |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud Detection (listings) | 10 | Reduce Cost (8) |
| Fraud Detection (transactions) | 10 | Reduce Cost (8) |
| "Because You Like" Explanations | 10 | Improve Discovery (5) |
| Accessibility Matching | 10 | Improve Discovery (7) |
| Content Moderation | 9 | Reduce Cost (10) |
| Spam Detection | 9 | Reduce Cost (9) |
| Review Quality Scoring | 9 | Improve Discovery (5) |
| Reputation Scoring | 9 | Increase Revenue (5) |

**Insight:** Trust capabilities almost always pair with either cost reduction (moderation/fraud) or discovery improvement (explanations/accessibility). A capability that ONLY improves trust without reducing cost or improving discovery is rare — and usually unjustifiable on its own.
